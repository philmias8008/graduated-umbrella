About

Offering the "human touch" in a rapidly advancing digital world

Handstart is not a corporation with account managers and junior handoffs. You deal with me directly, and I own the agreed upon output of our projects from start to finish.

Three things will always be clear: who you're working with, how long the work takes, and what it costs. For heavier website builds I recommend bringing in a developer I trust, same goes for my designer when it comes to intricate design work.

I don't take on every project. It has to be the right fit, for both of us. I would rather create real impact for you and build a relationship built on trust, rather than deliver cheap fixes and templates. I have no reason to upsell you: I recommend what fits your goals, not what grows the invoice.

Handstart began when the red tape of big companies became tiresome. Weeks of approvals delayed innovation and speed to market. I wanted to work the way work should actually move: I see it, we decide it, we start it.

That's why it's called Handstart. A helping human hand in a digital world that moves faster than most businesses can keep pace with. It might just be what you need to get a head start against your competition.

Based in Philadelphia, working everywhere.
