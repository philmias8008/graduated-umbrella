Rebrand & Website Campsite & hostel, Lombok

Five stars in person, but a question mark online

Before
After

Roy runs a top-rated place to stay in Sembalun, Lombok. 5 star Google reviews, 9.6 out of 10 on Hostelworld, first on the list for the whole valley. His business is an exceptionally unique experience in Lombok: a hostel-campsite in the mountains.

The challenge was that his website did not tell this story. Worse, it did not establish trust for potential guests during their consideration phase for booking. This was something I personally experienced when I went myself, and found other guests experienced after arriving.

Through the eyes of the prospective guest, the site did nothing to build confidence in the experience: location, safety, pricing. On the technical side, there was no funnel or clear user action, and no SEO or LLM strategy. The reputation was real. It just wasn’t conveyed anywhere that it could do the work of earning trust.

The foundation: The old name contained the word Rinjani which conflicted with many unrelated businesses and tours related to Mount Rinjani, which resulted in an unnecessary google ranking nightmare. The business was renamed Greenlife Sembalun and repositioned from "Rinjani camping" to a Sembalun basecamp in the hills. The site was rebuilt from scratch natively. The site is now multiple pages (there was only one before) and informational with a proper flow. Across both the site and HostelWorld, the copy is updated, baking in SEO and AI-search strategy on every page.

The system: A two-message WhatsApp flow that handles arrivals and asks for a review on the way out, so the online reputation continues to compound. Reviews are now front and center, across multiple platforms.

Pricing: with expansion and new hires future plans, we rebuilt the pricing model while competitive prices for his unique offering to the island.

Since re-launch, weekly metrics have increased: unique site visitors by 188%, total guest volume by 133%, and google reviews by 512% (compared to average weekly metrics during the old site and system). Both metrics as a result from the first and second themes in What We Did, and the decision in the third theme supported by no loss in demand or traffic.

All of this now driven by a trustworthy and visually cleaned-up website with clear paths to conversion and information flow for prospective guests. On the back-end, stronger SEO best practices to be found by Google and by AI and LLMs. The goal was not to add flashy animations or excessive content, rather to point consideration stage guests quickly to plentiful and reliable information, supported by CTAs. The place that everyone loved and loves now looks the part, digitally. I look forward to working with Roy now that his “base” is built, as we explore his long term expansion goals.
